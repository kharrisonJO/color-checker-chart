WCAG Contrast Ratio Analysis for Gimme Seaweed 2026

WCAG 2.1/2.2 color compliance requires a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text (18pt+/24px+ or 14pt+/18.5px+ bold) to meet Level AA, which is the standard for web accessibility. For higher, stricter AAA compliance, ratios of 7:1 (normal) and 4.5:1 (large) are required.

Large text is defined as 14 point (typically 18.66px) and bold or larger, or 18 point (typically 24px) or larger.

This means that in the matrix, if the intersection of two colors is green, they can be used together. If the intersection is red, then they cannot be used together.

For example, on the K-Crisps color matrix, text color of PMS 3529 C at 50% or 20% value passes with the background color PMS 7722 C.
